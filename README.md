**Airplane-Shooter** is a fast‑paced aerial combat game where you pilot a fighter plane through waves of enemy aircraft. Dodge bullets, unleash power‑ups, and clear the skies in this classic shoot‑’em‑up experience.

> *Take off, lock on, and dominate the skies.*

---

## 📖 Overview

Airplane-Shooter brings back the arcade feeling of vertical or horizontal scrolling shooters. Control your plane, shoot down enemy formations, collect upgrades, and survive increasingly difficult waves. The project is built with Unity and focuses on responsive controls, particle effects, and enemy AI patterns.

Current features (subject to change as development continues):
- Player‑controlled airplane with smooth movement and shooting
- Multiple enemy types (drones, bombers, jets)
- Power‑ups: rapid fire, shield, missile barrage
- Scoring system, health bar, and game over screen
- Procedural or wave‑based enemy spawning

---

## ✨ Features

- **Arcade action** – Fast, responsive controls and challenging bullet hell sections.
- **Varied enemies** – Different movement patterns, health, and attack styles.
- **Weapon upgrades** – Collect power‑ups to upgrade from single shot to spread shot or lasers.
- **Visual effects** – Explosions, trails, and screen shake for impact.
- **Mobile & desktop ready** – Supports keyboard, touch, or gamepad.
